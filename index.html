Elementary School Math

Grades 1–5: what we cover

Grade 1

Number foundations
  • Counting & numbers to 120
  • Addition & subtraction within 20
  • Tens and ones
  • Telling time & measuring
  • Shapes

Grade 2

Place value & fluency
  • Adding & subtracting to 1,000
  • Hundreds, tens, ones
  • Money & time
  • Measurement & data
  • Intro to multiplication

Grade 3

Multiply & divide
  • Multiplication & division facts
  • Intro to fractions
  • Area & perimeter
  • Rounding
  • Two-step word problems

Grade 4

Bigger numbers
  • Multi-digit multiplication
  • Long division
  • Equivalent fractions
  • Decimals
  • Angles & lines

Grade 5

Fractions & decimals
  • Adding, subtracting, multiplying fractions
  • Decimal operations
  • Volume
  • Coordinate plane
  • Order of operations
