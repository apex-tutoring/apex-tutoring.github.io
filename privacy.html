Privacy policy

Your family's privacy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

The short version: we collect only what we need to tutor your student and keep you updated. We never sell your information, never show ads, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Who we are

Apex Tutoring is a small math tutoring service run by Veer Shah with the support of family members. You can reach us at team.apextutoring@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • When you contact us: the parent or guardian's name and email, the student's grade, and your message.
  • When you book a call: the name, email, and time you choose in our booking calendar.
  • For the student portal: the parent or guardian's email (used as the login), the student's first name, grade, and course, and the session notes, progress, practice assignments, and upcoming session times your tutor records.
  • Placement quiz: quiz answers stay in your browser. Only the result is shared, and only if you choose to send it to us.

We don't collect home addresses, school names, payment card numbers, or any information we don't need for tutoring.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions and schedule sessions.
  • To plan lessons and track your student's progress.
  • To show you that progress in the student portal.

Students and children

Student portal accounts are created for, and managed by, a parent or guardian. We collect student information from the parent or guardian, not directly from children. A parent or guardian can review, correct, or delete their student's information at any time by emailing us.

Who can see it

  • Your tutor can see the information for students they teach.
  • In the portal, each family can see only their own student's information. This is enforced by our database security rules.
  • We use a few trusted services to run the website: Google Firebase (portal logins and data storage), Web3Forms (delivers contact-form messages to our email), Calendly (booking calendar), and GitHub Pages (website hosting). They process information only to provide those services.
  • We never sell or rent your information or share it for advertising.

How we protect it

Portal logins are password protected, and passwords are handled by Google Firebase. We never see or store them. Only your tutor can change student records.

How long we keep it

We keep portal records while your student is working with us. If you stop tutoring, or at any time you ask, we'll delete your account and your student's records.

Your choices

Email team.apextutoring@gmail.com to see, correct, or delete your family's information, or with any questions about this policy.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
